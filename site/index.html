03 / HOW TO READ THIS MAP

A collection, with boundaries.

The reading list contains 64 technical studies and five surveys. The fixed quantitative corpus includes 62 papers; this explorer exposes the 88 direct NL2SQL settings from 49 papers with detailed source records. A paper without mapped settings is not evidence of an absent capability.

The benchmark audit covers 12 selected settings from 10 studies. It describes inspected protocols and reporting. Neither this interface nor the recorded-result checks rerun models, certify interpretation validity, or establish deployment.
