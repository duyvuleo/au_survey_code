# Analysis inputs

These are fixed inputs to the submission's recorded-result checks. Files under an `outputs/` directory are retained results from the original analysis, not files generated by `analysis/run.py`. The default run reads them and writes recomputed results to `reproduced/` directories.

[Analysis scope](analysis-scope.json) records the 01 October 2026 publication cutoff and the fixed study membership. Literature-only additions do not enter the calculation inputs.

## Field conventions

- **Study and setting IDs:** paper IDs match the [reading list](../../papers/README.md). A `setting_id` identifies a particular study setting, rather than a model run or random seed.
- **Perspectives:** `I` = Interpretation, `B` = Answerability, `C` = Consequence, `A` = Action. The historical `B` code is retained so the records remain unchanged.
- **Assignment states:** `P` = confirmed present, `N` = absent within the inspected scope, `U` = unclear. Unclear evidence is retained when computing the smallest and largest possible overlap values.
- **Connections:** `integration` records information flow between perspectives; `relationship_evaluation` records an empirical examination of their relationship. These properties are independent.
- **CLARITY bundles:** `bundle_id` groups exact matching question/annotation records across conversation styles. Bundles do not establish independent user intents. The primary agreement population retains 319 column-ambiguity bundles after the declared screens.
- **CLARITY query outcomes:** hashes and result signatures identify recorded queries and outputs. Equality preserves full rows, column positions, multiplicity, and `NULL`; row order is considered when required by the declared contract. A failed query is not an empty result.
- **Benchmark comparisons:** AmbiQT and AMBROSIA retain native scoring diagnostics separately from stricter outcome checks. Results obtained under different comparison rules must be interpreted separately.

## Provenance and use

Published-table inputs retain source identifiers and transcription metadata where recorded. External replay identities are in [benchmark input hashes](../benchmarks/expected_sources.json) and [CLARITY input hashes](../clarity/expected_sources.py); the [frozen CLARITY protocol](../clarity/PROTOCOL.md) describes construction and comparison rules. Its executable copy remains in [frozen_protocol.py](../clarity/frozen_protocol.py). The replay guides record upstream revisions, dependencies, and access requirements.

The package contains derived records needed to check calculations, rather than upstream databases or model weights. Upstream materials retain their original terms. Recorded scientific assignments and review judgments remain evidence to inspect; passing the arithmetic checks does not certify their validity or complete human validation.

The relationship records reflect the approved human-source-review revision of 9 October 2026. Reviewer A and B are anonymous labels. The forms retain missing fields and original review qualifications. Personal verification was confirmed separately, without inferring completion dates or independence before discussion. The resolutions preserve the three initial disagreements. Source locators identify public documents and retained normalized-text positions; full papers are not redistributed.

The CLARITY semantic inspections were performed by an AI analyst. They are separate from the two-author relationship verification. The recorded-review checker verifies the stored summaries and does not validate the interpretations or repeat either review.

The source records also describe the LLM-assisted annotation procedure. Exact model snapshots and sampling settings are unavailable in the retained analysis records. The included criteria and fixed annotations support inspection and numerical recalculation, not exact regeneration of the agent annotations.
